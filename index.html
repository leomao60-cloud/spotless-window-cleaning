About Us

Fresno’s newest · Precision from day one

Solidify Window is Fresno’s newest window cleaning team—built from day one on precision, not shortcuts. Our trained technicians use pro-grade squeegees, scrubbers, and eco-friendly solutions to leave every pane streak-free—inside and out.

We serve residential neighborhoods and commercial properties across Fresno, CA, with flexible scheduling, fully insured crews, and a 100% satisfaction guarantee on every job.

From high-rise glass to exterior power washing, one trusted team handles it all—so you can focus on what matters while we make your property shine.
