About Us

For over 8 years, SPOTLESS has helped Fresno homeowners and businesses enjoy crystal-clear views. Our trained technicians use eco-friendly solutions and professional-grade tools to leave every pane streak-free—inside and out.

We serve residential neighborhoods and commercial properties across Fresno, CA, with flexible scheduling, fully insured crews, and a 100% satisfaction guarantee on every job.

From high-rise glass to solar arrays and exterior power washing, one trusted team handles it all—so you can focus on what matters while we make your property shine.
